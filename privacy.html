Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Short version: The Great Logout is hosted on our own server. The post generator runs in your browser. With your consent, we use privacy-conscious website statistics hosted on the same server. We do not use advertising trackers or profiling.

What data we collect

When you sign up for the email guide, we process the information you submit:

  • email address
  • optional first name
  • optional planned logout date
  • selected guide length
  • consent to receive the guide

For email delivery and unsubscribing, we also store technical records such as an unsubscribe token, signup time, unsubscribe time, scheduled email status, delivery message IDs, and delivery error messages where needed.

Why we use this data

We use this data to send the logout guide, follow-up check-ins, and related campaign emails you requested. The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

We may also process limited technical data to operate, secure, and improve the website and email system. The legal basis is our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Email guide and unsubscribe

You can unsubscribe from the email guide at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email. You can also contact us at support@thegreatlogout.org.

If you unsubscribe, we stop sending guide emails. We may keep a limited record of the unsubscribe status to avoid sending further emails and to document consent history.

Post generator

The post generator on the website runs in your browser. Text you type into the generator is used to create the downloadable image on your device.

Some email links can open the generator or request a downloadable SVG through the campaign API. In that case, the selected text, format, and color may be included in the URL request so the file can be generated.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the campaign:

  • Postmark, a service of AC PM LLC, for sending transactional and guide emails
  • Ko-fi, if you choose to support the campaign through the external contribution link

The website, signup service, subscriber database, email schedule, and website statistics are operated on our own server. Data required for email delivery is transferred to Postmark as our processor. See the Postmark privacy policy.

When you leave this website through an external link, the privacy policy of the external service applies.

Cookies and website statistics

With your explicit consent, we use self-hosted, privacy-conscious website statistics. They help us understand which pages, links, outreach campaigns, and campaign materials are useful. No data is transferred to an external analytics service.

We record page views, pseudonymous visitor and session identifiers, the referring domain, source and optional campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, and utm_term), broad device class, active visit time, link destinations, successful guide signups, and post downloads. Form contents, email addresses, full IP addresses, and full referring URLs are not stored in the analytics database.

The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and section 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021. Before you consent, no analytics events are sent and no analytics identifier is stored.

Your choice and pseudonymous visitor identifier are stored for up to 180 days in the first-party cookies tgl_analytics_consent and tgl_analytics_visitor. The session identifier is stored in your browser session storage and renewed after 30 minutes of inactivity. Individual analytics events are deleted after no more than 90 days.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time. Withdrawing consent deletes the visitor and current session identifiers from your device and stops further analytics events.

Server logs

When you visit this website, our server processes technical data such as IP address, request time, browser information, and the requested URL to deliver and protect the service and to diagnose errors. Server logs are not combined with the optional website statistics and are not used to profile visitors.

Retention

Email guide data is kept while your subscription is active. If you unsubscribe, we keep only what is needed to respect the unsubscribe request, maintain delivery records, and handle legal or operational questions. You can request deletion at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data. You may also withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact support@thegreatlogout.org. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the campaign, website, or email system changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
